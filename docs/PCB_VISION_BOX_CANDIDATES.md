# PCB-Vision component box candidates

`python scripts/build_pcbvision_box_candidates.py` reads the published, hash-verified RGB ZIP and derives YOLO-format **candidate** boxes from connected regions in its `General` semantic masks. Output goes to ignored `work/pcbvision-yolo-boxes/`, with distinct-image train/validation/test directories, label text files, a proposal manifest and `data.yaml`. The deterministic seed-42 split contains 33 training, 9 validation and 11 test PCBs. Pillow, NumPy and SciPy are required.

The 16-pixel minimum yields 2,384 candidate boxes over 53 external images: 1,248 IC, 442 capacitor and 694 connector regions. Counts are connected regions, not independently reviewed component instances. Direct visual inspection of the three overlays confirms that many boxes align with real ICs, capacitors or connector pads. The masks cover only those three semantic classes; other visible objects are unlabeled, and touching objects can merge while one object can fragment. Small regions below 16 pixels are discarded. The output is **not** complete detection ground truth, and no component-detector accuracy or training claim follows from it.

Image IDs, mask variant, source ZIP SHA-256, bounding boxes, component areas and the threshold are stored in the [published candidate manifest](../data/external/PCBVision/box-candidate-manifest.json). `python scripts/build_pcbvision_box_candidates.py --check` recomputes and compares every proposal to that manifest. General and Monoseg masks are two variants of the same 53 images, so they cannot form independent train and validation images. Review each intended class and group any correlated captures before using boxes for training or evaluation. These external images do not establish a Tokito board family, refdes, net, pin or Gerber link.

## First training smoke test

`python scripts/train_pcbvision_component_detector.py` trained a 20-epoch YOLO11n checkpoint at 512 pixels from the candidate boxes. The checkpoint, plots, per-epoch CSV and machine-readable [training report](../data/external/PCBVision/component-yolo-experiment/training-report.json) are published as an external-data experiment. The 11-PCB test split measured mean precision 0.2835, mean recall 0.1401, mAP50 0.1356 and mAP50-95 0.0419. Class mAP50-95 was 0.0824 for IC, 0.0432 for capacitor and 0.0 for connector.

These low results are useful failure evidence, not a finished component model. They show that the automatic connected-region conversion is insufficient for connector detection and weak for the other classes. The checkpoint is suitable only for a release-ineligible runtime smoke test. It remains private evaluation material under AGPL-3.0-only pending legal review. It does not replace reviewed Tokito photo labels or support claims about board, part, pin, BOM, Gerber, net or reference-designator recognition.
