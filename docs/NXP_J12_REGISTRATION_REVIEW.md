I inspected all 18 NXP photo files in the board annotation inventory, including the crop variants ([capture sheet](../previews/photo-pins/nxp-j12-capture-review.png)). Every capture shows the front of this board. The imported Rev C board has `170-90828 REV C` at board coordinate `(105.918, 1.74625)` on **B.Cu**, so these front photos cannot show that source-side revision inscription. No readable physical revision mark appears elsewhere in the available phone pixels. The J12 pin-1 numeral or another unambiguous physical orientation mark is also unreadable around the installed socket. CAD alignment suggests pin 1 at the lower right in the source image, but does not prove the photographed specimen follows that exact revision and orientation.

To promote the labels, photograph the back of this same specimen sharply enough to read its board revision, then photograph J12 closely enough to read pin 1 or a keyed orientation. Compare both with the original Rev C board and recheck the ten pixel boxes. The machine-readable [review record](../annotations/nxp-j12-registration-review.json) deliberately sets `training_eligible` to `false` until those checks pass.
